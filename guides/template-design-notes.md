# Template design notes (v1.0)

1. **The bracketed intake is deliberate.** Filling in name, course, professor, textbook, and exam dates is the student's first act of ownership, and it takes two minutes. Video 2 walks her through it on screen.
2. **The one rule is stated in her voice, not as a policy.** "You are training me for a room you cannot enter with me" gives the agent a reason it can repeat back when she pushes, which she will, at midnight, before the midterm. The rule bends only after genuine completion, which matches how a good human tutor behaves.
3. **The course pack is separate from this template.** This file is the personality and rules. The MIT 18.03 materials (notes, problem sets, past exams) get uploaded alongside it as reference files, with her own syllabus ranked above them. That keeps the template platform-agnostic and under every platform's instruction length limit.
4. **Repeat offenders is the exam bank inverted.** The fraternity kept a record of the professor's questions. She keeps a record of her own mistakes. By midterm week, hers is the more valuable document.
5. **Testable claim for the channel:** hand this template to a real student for two weeks and film what happens. That is Video 4.
