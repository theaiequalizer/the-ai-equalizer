# Start here
## Build your own tutor in ten minutes. Free. No signup with us, ever.

You watched the video. Now do the thing. You need your phone or a laptop, a pen, paper, and ten minutes. That is the whole initiation fee. Some students used to pay a lot more than that for a lot less than this.

---

### Step 1, Open an AI app (2 minutes)

Pick one. Any of these works, and the free account is enough to start:

- **Claude** (claude.ai)
- **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com)
- **Gemini** (gemini.google.com)

If you already have one on your phone, use that one. Done is better than perfect.

### Step 2, Make it a home, not a chat (1 minute)

Your tutor needs a permanent home so it remembers you. In Claude, create a **Project**. In ChatGPT, create a **Project** or a **custom GPT**. In Gemini, create a **Gem**. Name it: **My DiffEq Tutor**.

Do not skip this. A regular chat forgets you the moment you close it. The whole point of this tutor is that by week eight it knows your mistakes better than you do.

### Step 3, Copy the template (1 minute)

Press the copy button below. Paste everything into your project's instructions box (Claude calls it "Project instructions," ChatGPT calls it "Instructions," Gemini calls it "Instructions").

**[ COPY THE TEMPLATE ]**

### Step 4, Fill in five blanks (2 minutes)

At the top of what you pasted there are bracketed lines: your name, your school, your course and professor, your textbook, your exam dates. Fill them in. Skip what you do not know yet; you can add it later.

This is the moment it stops being our tutor and becomes yours.

### Step 5, Give it your course (2 minutes)

Upload your syllabus into the project. A photo of it works. If you have a problem set or a returned quiz, upload those too. Returned exams are gold: they are your professor's actual voice, and your tutor will study them.

No syllabus handy? Start anyway and add it tomorrow.

### Step 6, First session (right now, 5 minutes)

Grab the pen. Work any problem from your current homework, on paper, as far as you can get. Stuck is fine. Wrong is fine. Then photograph the page, send the photo, and type one word:

**check**

Watch what happens. It will find where your work goes wrong, tell you what kind of mistake it is, and ask you a question so you can fix it yourself. It will not give you the answer. It is not allowed to. You are training for a room it cannot enter with you.

---

### The seven words you now own

- **check** + photo: find my error, do not fix it
- **stuck** + photo: one hint, just one
- **why**: explain the idea under this step, plain words first
- **drill**: give me another one like it
- **harder**: I got it, raise the bar
- **exam me**: build me a timed quiz from my own weak spots
- **log**: end the session, write down what tripped me

End every session with **log**. That log becomes a record of your own mind, and by midterm week it will be the most valuable document you own.
