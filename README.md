# The AI Equalizer

**You were never behind. You were uninformed.**

For decades, some engineering students had a private network: exam banks, tutors who knew them, older students who explained things in words they could use. The rest sat in the same classrooms believing they were competing on ability. This repository is the network, opened to everyone.

Here you will find everything you need to build your own AI study tutor: free, in your own account, in about ten minutes.

## Start here

**If you are a student:** go straight to the [Start Here page](https://theaiequalizer.github.io/the-ai-equalizer/). Seven steps, ten minutes, zero dollars.

## What is in this repository

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `docs/` | The Start Here page, the six-step build for students |
| `template/` | The tutor instruction template you paste into your AI app |
| `course-packs/` | Subject playbooks the tutor teaches from (differential equations first; more coming) |
| `guides/` | The full build walkthrough with the exemplar course (MIT 18.03) |

## How to get the files

You do not need to know anything about GitHub.

- **To copy the template:** open `template/tutor-template.md`, press the **copy raw file** button (the two-squares icon at the top right of the file view), and paste into your AI app.
- **To download everything:** press the green **Code** button at the top of this page, then **Download ZIP**. Unzip it anywhere. The files are plain text; any app can open them.

## The rules of this house

- This is free and it stays free. Nobody will ever charge you to reach your tutor.
- Your data is yours. Everything you upload lives in your own account on the AI app you chose. This project never sees it.
- The tutor's one rule: it never gives you the answer to a problem you are working on. It trains you for a room it cannot enter with you.
- MIT OpenCourseWare materials are referenced by link, never rehosted. Follow their license as we follow it.
